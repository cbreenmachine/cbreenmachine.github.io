To prepare for a role as an AI and Plant Biology Resident at Google[x] that I would start in March, I wanted to learn about agriculutral systems. I was inpsired to start with books covering the cultural dimensions of agriculture by a good friend, Sebastian[^1].   

# Agriculture

* *Wizard and Prophet* by Charles C. Mann. One of my favorite books. Great historical telling of the green revolution, biographies of two key agricultaral figures (Norman Borlaug and William Vogt).

* *Unsettling of America* by Wendell Berry. Another classic. Berry is critical of modern agriculture and the innovation driven by "Big Ag". 

# Cultural 

* Omnivore's Dilema

* *Eating to Extinction* by Dan Saladino. An anthology covering both common and exotic foodstuffs. In addition to learning some fun plant trivia (apples are from Kazahkstan), I definitely gained an appreciation for local varieties. A certain amount of decentralization seems key to keeping the artisanship in plant breeding...

* Third Plate

# Start-Ups

* *Power Law* by Sebastian Mallaby. Great history of Silicon Valley and much of its mythos.

* *Zero to One* by Peter Thiel and Blake Masters.
